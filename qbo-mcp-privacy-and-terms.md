# Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026

## Privacy Policy

This application ("Application") is a privately operated software integration used by its owner to access and manage the owner's own QuickBooks Online account. The Application is not offered as a service to the general public.

### Information Accessed

The Application may access information available through the QuickBooks Online API, including accounting, transaction, vendor, customer, receipt, expense, invoice, and related financial information.

The Application may also use OAuth authentication information, including access tokens and refresh tokens, as necessary to connect to QuickBooks Online.

### Use of Information

Information obtained from QuickBooks Online is used only to provide the functionality requested by the owner of the Application, including retrieving, reviewing, creating, or updating accounting information.

QuickBooks data is not sold, rented, marketed, or provided to unrelated third parties.

### Data Storage

The Application may temporarily or persistently store authentication credentials, OAuth tokens, configuration information, logs, or QuickBooks data where necessary for operation of the Application.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored credentials and data from unauthorized access.

### Third-Party Services

The Application communicates with Intuit and QuickBooks Online services. Information provided to or retrieved from QuickBooks Online is also subject to Intuit's applicable privacy policies, terms, and security practices.

### Data Sharing

The Application does not intentionally disclose QuickBooks data to third parties except when necessary to communicate with QuickBooks Online or when required by law.

### Data Retention and Deletion

Information retained by the Application may be deleted by the Application owner when it is no longer required. OAuth authorization may also be revoked through the applicable Intuit or QuickBooks account settings.
